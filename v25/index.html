01 · whereami · residential proxy detection

One question, answered well.

Attackers now rent real home internet connections so their logins look like your users: the right ISP, the right country, one attempt per address. Data-center blocklists and geo rules wave them through. whereami tells you whether a login’s IP is one of those rented exits. What you do about it stays in your code.
