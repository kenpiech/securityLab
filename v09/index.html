How we evaluate.

Accuracy claims in security are cheap. These are the rules we hold ourselves to.

01

Labeled by outcome

Attempts are labeled by what happened next (confirmed takeover, chargeback, user report), not by our own model.

02

Held-out time windows

We train on past months and test on later ones, so scores reflect attackers we hadn’t seen yet.

03

Published and reproducible

Dataset description, metrics code, and quarterly results are public.
