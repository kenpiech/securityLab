Introduction

We study how modern systems are attacked, from industrial devices to AI agents, and we turn that understanding into precise, well-made tools for the people who defend them.

Practice
  • i.

    Vulnerability research

    Deep analysis of software, firmware, and hardware, reported responsibly and published in full.

  • ii.

    Threat intelligence

    Sustained study of the groups targeting critical industries: who they are, what they use, and what they want.

  • iii.

    AI system security

    Rigorous testing of models and agents for manipulation, leakage, and misuse.

  • iv.

    Adversary emulation

    Discreet, objective-driven red team engagements with senior researchers.
