Integrate

One call. One field. Your rule.

whereami plugs into the auth you already run. It doesn’t replace your login or decide for you. It answers one question well.

  • Call it after the password or magic-link check, before issuing a session.
  • Read residential_proxy and confidence.
  • Apply your own policy: MFA, delay, or log. Plain REST, so there’s nothing to be locked into.
