For a century, fire lookouts kept watch over the backcountry from towers on the highest peaks. One person, a map, and a good view could save a whole forest by seeing trouble early.

securityLab works the same way for networks. Our researchers study the terrain, learn how intrusions start, and build tools that raise the alarm at the first wisp of smoke.
